My Redmi Buds 6 Lite long-press touch opens the assistant, and there's no way to turn it off — the only choices are "Open assistant" or "Make a call". Disable calls and I lose call support; keep it and I launch Google every single time I adjust my earbuds.

So I made an assistant that does nothing. Set Nossist as your default assistant and the long-press hits a dead end: no UI, no stolen focus, no animation. Just silence.
